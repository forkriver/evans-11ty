When the Markowitz brothers pitched their idea for a "pseudo-horror" TV show for kids, they promised a celebrity would be a part of it: Vincent Price. A contract was signed for 130 hour-long episodes and things seemed great.

There was only one problem: *They didn't know Vincent!*

Discover the fascinating journey of how *The Hilarious House of Frightenstein*, the quirky 1970's Canadian cult-classic, began.
